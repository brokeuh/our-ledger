Backup & restore

Your data lives only in this browser, on this device — nothing is sent anywhere. Download a backup file to keep it safe or move it to a new phone, and restore from one to load it back in. Restoring replaces everything currently in the app.

Add entry

Doesn't change your totals — just a label for your own reference.
It will be added to every month automatically, like rent or electricity, until you stop or remove it.

Add person

Enter the net (take-home) hourly rate. Vacation and ADV days are paid in full by the employer; sick days use the % above. Special leave (e.g. parental leave via RVA) is paid by someone else at a flat daily rate instead of your wage. A home office day pays full wage and still earns food vouchers, just no travel allowance — add an allowance above if your employer pays one for working from home. Travel and food vouchers otherwise only count on days actually worked at the workplace.

Mark hours off

For a few hours off on an otherwise normal work day. Unpaid hours are deducted from wage at the hourly rate; commute and food vouchers aren't affected.

Mark a day off

Travel never counts on a day off. Food vouchers stop too, except on a home office day. Sick days use the % set on this person; ADV and vacation are paid in full; unpaid days are paid nothing; special leave (e.g. parental leave via RVA) pays the flat daily amount set on this person instead of wage; home office pays full wage, still earns food vouchers, and adds any home-office allowance you've set.

Starting savings balance

Enter what you actually have saved right now, and which month that's true as of. From there, the app adds what each month saves (or subtracts what it loses) automatically, plus or minus anything you mark as paid from savings.
